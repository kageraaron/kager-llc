# GEMINI.md: Text to Speech Generator

## Project Vision
To provide a fast, offline-capable text-to-speech generator for accessibility or quick audio creation needs.

## Technical Preferences
- **Frontend:** Vanilla JavaScript, HTML, CSS.
- **Processing:** Native JavaScript `window.speechSynthesis` API.
- **Deployment:** Static hosting.

## Monetization Strategy
- **Ad Banners:** Display banner ads.
- **SEO Focus:** Optimize for "text to speech generator", "client-side text reader", "browser speech synthesis".
