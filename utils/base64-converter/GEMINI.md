# GEMINI.md: Base64 Converter

## Project Vision
To provide a fast, secure, client-side tool for developers to encode and decode Base64 strings.

## Technical Preferences
- **Frontend:** Vanilla JavaScript, HTML, CSS.
- **Processing:** `btoa()` and `atob()`.

## Monetization Strategy
- **Ad Banners:** Display banner ads.
- **SEO Focus:** Optimize for "base64 encode", "base64 decode", "base64 converter".
