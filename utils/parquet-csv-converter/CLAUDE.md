# CLAUDE.md: Parquet to CSV Converter

## User Experience
The tool must be straightforward: upload a file, select conversion direction, and download the result. Since Parquet files can be large, providing a progress indicator or a small preview of the data (first 10 rows) is crucial for a good UX.

## Aesthetic Considerations
- **Theme:** Professional, data-centric, and clean. Use a dark or neutral theme that suggests a "developer tool" or "data workbench" feel.
- **Typography:** Monospace fonts for data previews and status messages. Clean sans-serif for UI labels and instructions.
- **Layout:** Centered workspace with clear drop zones. Results and previews should be well-organized in tables. Ensure ad placements do not obstruct the conversion workflow but remain highly visible.
