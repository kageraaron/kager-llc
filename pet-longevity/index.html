The Modern Pet Health Crisis

🦴

Cancer Epidemic

Roughly 1 in 4 dogs will develop cancer in their lifetime. Among dogs over 10, cancer accounts for nearly half of all deaths — far higher than in their wild ancestors.

⚖

Obesity Pandemic

An estimated 56% of dogs and 60% of cats in the US are overweight or obese. Carb-heavy kibble drives chronic insulin spikes that wild canids and felids never evolved to handle.

🔥

Ultra-Processed Diet

Most kibble is extruded at 200–300°F, denaturing proteins and creating advanced glycation end products (AGEs) — compounds linked to inflammation, kidney disease, and cancer.

🦷

Dental & Gut Decay

By age 3, 80% of dogs and 70% of cats have periodontal disease. Soft processed food provides no mechanical cleaning and feeds inflammatory oral bacteria.

Foundation

What Species-Appropriate Actually Means

Dogs are facultative carnivores; cats are obligate carnivores. Their digestive anatomy hasn't meaningfully changed in 14,000+ years. Here's what evolutionary biology tells us they need.

🥩

The 80/10/10 Framework

The Prey Model Raw (PMR) breakdown approximates the composition of a whole prey animal:

ComponentRatio
Muscle Meat80%
Edible Bone10%
Organ Meat10% (½ liver)

BARF (Biologically Appropriate Raw Food) adds ~10% vegetables, fruit, and seeds. Cats require pure PMR — they cannot efficiently use plant matter.

🌾

Why Carbohydrates Matter

Cancer cells preferentially metabolize glucose (the Warburg effect). Most kibble is 30–60% carbohydrate by dry weight.

Carb load comparison:

  1. Wild canine prey diet: ~5–8% carbs
  2. BARF raw diet: ~5–15% carbs
  3. Premium grain-free kibble: ~30–40% carbs
  4. Standard kibble: 40–60% carbs (mostly corn, rice, wheat)

Ketogenic diets are being studied as adjunct cancer therapy in dogs (KetoPet Sanctuary; CSU Flint Animal Cancer Center).

🐟

Critical Fatty Acids

Modern commercial pet food is heavily skewed toward inflammatory omega-6 (from corn/poultry fat). The wild diet maintains a ratio closer to 1:1 to 1:4 (omega-3:omega-6).

  • EPA + DHA — anti-inflammatory; reduces tumor growth in canine cancer models
  • Sardines / mackerel — best whole-food source; 1 sardine 2–3× per week
  • Avoid: high-omega-6 oils (corn, sunflower, soy)

Target ratio: 1:1 to 1:5 omega-3:omega-6. Most kibble runs 1:15 or worse.

Evidence Caveat

Most supplement evidence in pets comes from small-scale veterinary trials, observational studies, and extrapolation from human or rodent research. Effects are real but modest. Diet and weight management have far stronger longevity evidence than any supplement. Always discuss new supplements with your veterinarian — particularly if your pet takes medications, has kidney/liver disease, or is undergoing cancer treatment.

Research & Evidence Base

The peer-reviewed science behind raw feeding and pet longevity. Links open PubMed or source publications.

Cancer Epidemiology

Diet & Canine Cancer Risk

A long-running observational study by Dr. Gerard Lippert tracking 522 client-owned dogs found that homemade fresh-food diets were associated with significantly longer lifespans than commercial-only diets, with multivariate adjustment for breed and weight.

Lippert & Sapy — Lifespan & Diet in Dogs →
Processing

AGEs in Pet Food

A 2018 University of Helsinki analysis quantified advanced glycation end products (AGEs) in commercial pet foods. Dry extruded foods contained 122× the dietary AGE load of the BARF-equivalent raw diet, with implications for inflammation and aging.

Hemida et al. — AGEs in Pet Food (PubMed) →
Cancer Adjunct

Turkey Tail Mushroom in Hemangiosarcoma

A Penn Vet randomized trial in dogs with splenic hemangiosarcoma reported the longest median survival times reported for the disease in dogs receiving high-dose I'm-Yunity (PSP) — a polysaccharopeptide from Coriolus versicolor (turkey tail mushroom).

Brown & Reetz (2012) — PubMed →
Omega-3

EPA/DHA in Canine Osteoarthritis

A multi-center, double-blinded, randomized trial demonstrated that diets supplemented with high concentrations of fish-oil-derived omega-3 fatty acids significantly improved owner-assessed mobility and reduced pain scores in arthritic dogs.

Roush et al. (2010) — JAVMA →
Microbiome

Raw Diet & Gut Microbiome

Research from the University of Helsinki and others shows that raw-fed dogs maintain higher microbial diversity, including more Lactobacillus and fewer pathogenic Clostridium, compared to kibble-fed dogs — with implications for systemic inflammation and immune health.

Search PubMed: raw diet microbiome dog →
Caloric Restriction

Lifelong Caloric Restriction in Labs

Purina's 14-year lifetime study of 48 Labrador Retrievers showed dogs fed 25% less than free-choice littermates lived a median of 1.8 years longer (~15% lifespan extension) and developed clinical osteoarthritis 2 years later. Body condition is a stronger lever than diet composition.

Kealy et al. (2002) — JAVMA →
