2C-B · phenethylamine psychedelic

2C-B Harm Reduction:
Test, Dose Low, Mind Your Stack.

2C-B is a long-lasting psychedelic phenethylamine with stimulating edges — common at parties, often in powder or pressed pills. Nothing here replaces a milligram scale, reagent testing, and checking for opioids in the supply.

Test before you ingest Dose context
⚠

This is not medical advice. 2C-B is illegal in many jurisdictions. If someone is overheating, confused, or non-responsive, seek emergency care.

Critical risks

☠

Wrong drug / dose

Looks like flour or fits in a capsule does not mean it is 2C-B or the dose you think. Fentanyl has appeared in unrelated powders — use opioid test strips on powders whenever the supply is unregulated.

💔

Stimulant layering

Party stacks (MDMA, cocaine, meth, caffeine energy drinks) stack cardiovascular and thermal load with a psychedelic — higher heat-stroke and panic risk.

🚫

MAOIs & contraindications

Many harm reduction references flag MAOI co-use as dangerous with phenethylamines. When on prescription psych meds, research interactions — don’t guess.

🌡

Heat & sensory overload

Combined with dancing and crowd density, nausea and overheating happen. Plan cool-down breaks and electrolyte-containing fluids.

Step 1

Reagents & strips

Reagents show presence, not purity or mg strength. Use a milligram scale for anything you plan to put in your body.

🧪

Marquis · Mecke · Simon's

2C-B is often discussed with Marquis turning yellow–green (not MDMA-purple). Always read against the chart that shipped with your kit — lighting and sample size change how colors photograph.

Weigh it

Eyeballing phenethylamines is how people take multiples of what they meant to. A 0.001 g scale with calibration weights is non-negotiable for powders.

Step 2

Dose ranges (oral, approximate)

Community reports cluster lower for combined settings. Purity and individual sensitivity vary wildly — start low with any new batch.

Step 3

Set, setting, sitter

Sensory intensity and duration (often ~6–10 h depending on dose and person) mean you want a manageable environment and a calm friend who is largely sober.

Party mitigation

  • Pre-plan water + electrolytes; alternate dancing with cool air
  • Agree on a meet-up point — phones die and crowds shift
  • Know how you’ll leave if you’re overwhelmed (quiet room, outside air)

Don’t mix blindly

Avoid stacking with SSRIs/MAOIs without medical guidance. Avoid adding more stimulants if your heart is racing — cool down first.

Further reading

Scientific literature on 2C-B specifically is thinner than for classic psychedelics in therapy trials — prioritize organized reference sources and analytical data.
