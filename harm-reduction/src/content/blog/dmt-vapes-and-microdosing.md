A DMT vape cartridge is a sealed container of unknown liquid, and that is the whole problem. Crystal you can look at, weigh, and scrape onto a reagent plate. A cart allows none of that. The label says "DMT," but the two tryptamines sold under that name, N,N-DMT and 5-MeO-DMT, are different drugs and are not active at the same dose. 5-MeO-DMT works at a fraction of the N,N-DMT dose and has appeared in fatal toxicology cases, and confusing the two is the mistake that hurts people. A pen also changes how the drug arrives: small repeated puffs instead of one large hit, which makes overshoot easy.

---

## Quick answers

**Can I tell what is in a DMT cart?** Not without a lab. Reagent tests are presumptive, and FTIR struggles with diluted solutions ([PMID 41087826](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41087826/)). A cart is a diluted solution.

**Is 5-MeO-DMT just a stronger DMT?** No. Different molecule, highest affinity at 5-HT1A rather than 5-HT2A ([PMID 35149998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35149998/)), different experience, much smaller effective dose.

**Does an Ehrlich kit answer this?** No. Ehrlich turns purple for indoles, and N,N-DMT, 5-MeO-DMT, psilocybin and LSD are all indoles.

**Is microdosing DMT studied?** Barely. No controlled trial of repeated low-dose vaped N,N-DMT exists. The nearest evidence is a phase 1 trial of sublingual 5-MeO-DMT ([PMID 40659913](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40659913/)).

**Most useful precaution?** Sit or lie down before you inhale, with a sober person present. Onset is seconds.

## N,N-DMT and 5-MeO-DMT are not interchangeable

N,N-dimethyltryptamine is the tryptamine in ayahuasca and in the classic smoked experience. In human dose-response work, intravenous N,N-DMT began almost immediately, peaked at 90 to 120 seconds, and largely resolved by 30 minutes, with hallucinogenic effects only at the two highest doses ([PMID 8297217](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8297217/)).

5-methoxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine is a different compound, weighted toward 5-HT1A ([PMID 35149998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35149998/)), usually described as overwhelming and largely non-visual rather than kaleidoscopic. The dose gap is the dangerous part. Trials of vaporized 5-MeO-DMT used 6, 12 and 18 mg, with 12 mg already producing a full experience ([PMID 37409159](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37409159/)), and a case report of vaporized toad secretion estimated only 5 to 7 mg of active 5-MeO-DMT for a peak ([PMID 30471678](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30471678/)). Both are far below a full smoked N,N-DMT dose. No head-to-head human potency comparison exists, so treat the ratio as approximate and the direction as certain: less 5-MeO-DMT does more.

It has also killed. A 25-year-old man died after drinking a preparation containing beta-carbolines and tryptamines; heart blood held 1.88 mg/L of 5-MeO-DMT against 0.02 mg/L of N,N-DMT, and the death was ruled hallucinogenic amine intoxication ([PMID 16356341](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16356341/)). Forensic casework has also identified 5-MeO-DMT at over 95% purity among unlabeled research chemical powders ([PMID 34049075](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34049075/)). Take a lungful sized for N,N-DMT from a cart holding 5-MeO-DMT and you have taken several times a full dose of a drug you did not expect.

## You cannot verify what is in a cartridge

The contents are an unknown concentration in an undisclosed carrier, the housing is not built to open, and any sample you extract is diluted, the condition that degrades colorimetric and portable spectroscopic methods alike ([PMID 41087826](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41087826/)). No published analysis of DMT cart contents exists. Survey work confirms people vape DMT in e-cigarette devices ([PMID 37632360](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37632360/)), but nothing tells you what is in the products, so anyone quoting a typical concentration or adulterant is guessing.

## Why vaping changes the dose curve

Smoked DMT works the way it does because of how it is taken: a large dose over a few seconds, past the threshold in one move. Low doses of N,N-DMT were emotional and body-focused rather than hallucinogenic, and the low-intermediate dose produced the least desirable effects of any dose tested ([PMID 8297217](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8297217/)). A pen parks you in that region.

- **Partial experiences become the norm**: onset, body load and anxiety without arrival.
- **Dose stacking is invisible**: each puff delivers an unknown amount, and the last has not declared itself before the next. In a survey of 515 people who use 5-MeO-DMT, 39% redosed in the same session ([PMID 29708042](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29708042/)), and a pen makes that easier than a pipe does.

## Temperature, and what is not established

DMT needs enough heat to vaporize and not so much that it burns, which is why traditional users favour low, gentle heat. Beyond that, the specifics are not well characterised. We searched for thermal degradation data on DMT and found no study establishing a degradation threshold, a breakdown product profile, or a validated coil temperature. If someone quotes you the correct temperature for vaping DMT, it is not from the literature. Carts also need an undisclosed thinning agent to flow, and inhaled solvent exposure is its own hazard.

## The physical safety issue

Effects peak inside two minutes ([PMID 8297217](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8297217/)). You will not have time to sit down or put the device somewhere safe. People drop lit devices and slump sideways.

- **Be seated or lying down before you inhale**, not after. Floor level beats a chair.
- **Have a sober sitter** to take the device from your hand and keep your airway clear.
- **Never standing**, and never near stairs, balconies, roads, fire or water. Drowning in shallow water is a real outcome.
- **No driving** for the rest of the session.

## Microdosing DMT: the evidence is thin

There is no published randomized controlled trial of repeated low-dose vaped or smoked N,N-DMT in humans. Not a weak one, not a small one. Claims that it improves mood, focus or creativity rest on anecdote and analogy.

The analogy is where people go wrong. Psilocybin microdosing has a real literature, and its most rigorous parts point toward expectation rather than pharmacology, covered in our [psilocybin microdosing guide](/blog/microdosing-psilocybin.html) and our post on [whether microdosing is safe long term](/blog/is-microdosing-safe-long-term.html). None of it transfers to DMT: different receptor engagement, duration, route and tolerance.

The nearest trial is not N,N-DMT: a phase 1 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of weekly sublingual 5-MeO-DMT at 6, 9 or 12 mg over four weeks, which was tolerated and shifted brain activity without full psychedelic effects ([PMID 40659913](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40659913/)). That is safety data for a different molecule by a different route. Microdosing also needs a repeatable dose, and a cart cannot tell you what it delivers per puff.

## The MAOI question

Oral DMT is destroyed by monoamine oxidase before it reaches the brain, which is why ayahuasca pairs it with MAOI-containing plants and why some people add an isolated MAOI. That changes the risk picture.

A 2026 review of 219 publications on MAOI interactions documented suspected serotonin toxicity with MDMA, amphetamine, dextromethorphan and tramadol, fatalities with MDMA and 5-MeO-DMT, and rhabdomyolysis with DMT and 5-MeO-DMT ([PMID 41546846](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41546846/)). The warning about ayahuasca-type preparations with SSRIs was raised decades ago and still stands ([PMID 9924842](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9924842/)).

If you take an SSRI, SNRI, tramadol, dextromethorphan, triptans or lithium, do not add an MAOI, and keep MDMA far from one. See our [drug interactions guide](/interactions.html) and our post on [serotonin syndrome](/blog/serotonin-syndrome.html). An MAOI also intensifies whatever tryptamine is present, so if the material was 5-MeO-DMT it amplifies the error.

## What testing can and cannot tell you

Ehrlich reagent turns purple for indoles, and N,N-DMT, 5-MeO-DMT, psilocybin, psilocin and LSD are all indoles. Purple means an indole is present and nothing more: it cannot separate N,N-DMT from 5-MeO-DMT, or either from LSD or a mushroom extract. Reagent testing does not solve the problem this post is about, and no reagent on the market does.

It answers the reverse question on a solid sample: whether an indole is present at all. ## The takeaway

A cart is a sealed unknown, and the unknown that hurts people is whether it holds N,N-DMT or 5-MeO-DMT, a drug active at a much smaller dose that has appeared in fatal cases. No reagent separates them. If you use anyway: sit or lie down first, keep a sober sitter, take one inhalation and wait several minutes before another, stay clear of water and stairs, and skip it if you are on an SSRI, SNRI or MAOI.
