You cannot tell by looking, crushing, tasting or smelling. **Appearance, colour, texture, taste and smell carry no reliable information about what is in a crystal or a pressed pill.** Big chunky crystals mean the batch was recrystallised carefully and handled gently, nothing more. A crisp pill with a sharp logo means somebody owned a press and a stamp die, both ordinary industrial purchases. Only chemistry answers the question: Marquis to screen for the MDMA class, two-part Simon's to separate MDMA from MDA, Froehde to confirm, and a fentanyl test strip run separately, because no reagent detects fentanyl at the doses that kill. About ten minutes on a plate.

## Quick answers

**Can you tell fake molly by taste?** No. MDMA is bitter, and so are cathinones, methamphetamine and most of what gets sold in its place.

**Do big crystals mean it is pure?** No. Crystal size reflects recrystallisation and handling. Fine powder can be clean, and large clear rocks can be a cathinone.

**Does the pill press or stamp mean anything?** No. Presses and dies are commodity equipment, and popular logos get copied within weeks. A logo is branding, not a certificate.

**What does work?** Marquis, then Simon's A and B, then Froehde, then a fentanyl strip on a separate dissolved sample. The [DanceSafe MDMA kit](/mdma-kit) contains those reagents. *Some links below are affiliate links; we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.*

**What can reagents not tell me?** Dose, potency, purity, or everything in a mixture.

## Why the physical clues everyone repeats are worthless

Every scene has a folk taxonomy: brown crystal is "dirty," champagne rock is "the good stuff," white powder is "cut." None of it survives contact with how the substance is made and moved.

**Colour** comes from residual solvent, oxidation, or an impurity from the last recrystallisation. Off-white, beige, tan, brown and grey MDMA all exist, and so do off-white, beige and brown cathinones.

**Crystal size** comes from cooling rate and handling. Slow cooling grows big crystals, a fast crash grows small ones, and a bag that spent weeks in a pocket arrives as powder regardless. Cathinones like methylone form large attractive shards readily, one reason they sell as "high grade molly."

**Smell and taste.** The sassafras association comes from precursor chemistry, not the finished compound, and bitterness is near universal here. The taste test also requires putting an unidentified substance in your mouth, which is a dose.

**Pill appearance.** A familiar logo tells you the die exists, not who used it. Pills sold under one stamp in the same city in the same month have been found to contain different substances at wildly different amounts, and two pills from one bag can differ in dose. See [ecstasy pills versus molly](/blog/ecstasy-vs-molly-mdma.html).

## The protocol that does answer the question

Scrape a few grains onto a white ceramic [spot plate](/spot-plate), using a separate scrape and well for each reagent. Add one or two drops, watch the first 60 seconds, and read under neutral light rather than club lighting.

### Step 1: Marquis screens for the class

**MDMA and MDA both go purple, deepening to black**, usually within seconds.

| What is in the sample | Marquis result |
|---|---|
| MDMA | Purple to black |
| MDA | Purple to black |
| Methamphetamine | Orange to brown |
| Amphetamine | Orange to brown |
| Most cathinones | Yellow, or no reaction |
| Caffeine | No reaction |
| Ketamine | No reaction |

The useful part is the negative. If your "molly" sits there yellow, orange or unchanged, it is not MDMA, and you learned that before taking any of it. Our [guide to reading a Marquis result](/blog/how-to-read-a-marquis-reagent-result.html) covers the edge cases.

### Step 2: Simon's separates MDMA from MDA

This is why a dedicated MDMA kit exists rather than a single bottle. Marquis cannot split MDMA from MDA, since both run purple to black. **Simon's is the discriminator.** Add **Simon's A first, then Simon's B** on top, on a fresh scrape.

- **MDMA**: turns blue, usually a strong deep blue within about 30 seconds
- **MDA**: stays clear, colourless or very pale, with no blue development

Simon's responds to secondary amines, and MDMA is a secondary amine while MDA is a primary amine. Full colour reference in our [Simon's reagent chart](/blog/simons-reagent-color-chart.html).

The distinction is practical. MDA hits harder, runs noticeably longer, and strains the body across that longer window, so dosing and redosing it on an MDMA schedule makes for a rough night. See [MDA versus MDMA](/blog/mda-vs-mdma.html).

### Step 3: Froehde confirms

Froehde takes MDMA dark purple to black and gives different colours for several look-alikes that muddy a Marquis read, so it works as a second opinion.

## Fentanyl strips are a separate test, and they read backwards

No reagent detects fentanyl: not Marquis, Simon's, Froehde, or any colour test. Fentanyl is active in the low microgram range, far below the material a reagent needs to produce visible colour. Immunoassay strips are the only home tool that looks for it, and dilution matters, because strips built for urine throw false results if the solution is too concentrated:

1. Take roughly **10 mg**, a small scrape, not a dose you intend to take.
2. Dissolve it in about **5 mL of water**, roughly a teaspoon.
3. Dip the strip to the fill line for about **15 seconds**.
4. Lay it flat and read at **2 to 5 minutes**, not at 30 seconds and not an hour later.

Now the part people get backwards. **One line is positive. Two lines is negative.**

- **Two lines** (control plus test line, even a faint one): no fentanyl detected
- **One line** (control only): fentanyl detected, do not use
- **No lines**: invalid, run another strip

A faint second line still counts as two and still reads negative. Full walkthrough in our [fentanyl test strip guide](/blog/how-to-use-fentanyl-test-strips.html), and [strips are here](/fentanyl-strips). Contamination is often unevenly distributed through a bag, so a negative on one scrape does not clear the batch.

## What reagents cannot tell you, ever

**Dose or potency.** A colour is a yes or no, not a number. A pill giving textbook purple-black could hold 80 mg or 250 mg. Read the [MDMA dosing guide](/blog/mdma-dosing-guide.html) and start low regardless of how clean the test looked.

**Purity.** A sample that is 30% MDMA and 70% caffeine gives the same confident purple as one that is 95% MDMA.

**Everything in a mixture.** Darker reactions mask lighter ones. With MDMA and a cathinone both present, the MDMA reaction dominates and you see purple to black exactly as if the cathinone were absent. That is why a positive reduces risk rather than eliminating it.

## What "fake molly" usually turns out to be

Checking services publish what they find, and the same short list keeps appearing. An analysis of drugs bought from cryptomarkets and submitted to an Australian service documented adulteration and outright substitution across drug types ([PMID 38437019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38437019/)).

**Synthetic cathinones.** Methylone, eutylone, ethylone, MDPV, mephedrone. Cheap, attractive crystals, and a stimulant push that reads as a weak roll. Shorter acting, which drives compulsive redosing, with more risk of agitation, multi-day insomnia and hyperthermia. Most stay yellow or unreactive on Marquis.

**Methamphetamine.** Marquis orange to brown. A long stimulant plateau with none of the empathogenic quality, and far more dependence risk.

**Caffeine, fillers, or nothing active.** MSM, sugar and caffeine are all unreactive on Marquis.

**MDA sold as MDMA.** The substitution reagents almost miss: identical Marquis reaction, separated only by Simon's. Stronger and longer, so your dose plan is wrong.

For a fuller read, check whether a [drug checking service](/where-to-get-drugs-tested.html) operates near you: lab mass spectrometry identifies components and rough proportions.

## The bottom line

Your eyes, nose and tongue cannot answer "is my molly real," and neither can the logo on the pill. Chemistry can, partly. Run **Marquis** to screen for the class, **Simon's A then B** to separate MDMA from MDA, **Froehde** to confirm, then a **fentanyl strip** on a separate dissolved sample, remembering that one line means positive. The result is a strong signal about identity and silent about dose, purity and mixtures, so start low regardless.
