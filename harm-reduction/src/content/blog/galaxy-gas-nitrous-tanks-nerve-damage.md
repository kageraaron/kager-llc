Galaxy Gas is not chemically different from the nitrous oxide in a whipped cream charger. What changed is the container. A classic whippet charger holds 8 grams. The flavored tanks that went viral in 2024 hold 580 grams and up, roughly 72 chargers in one purchase costing less than a bar tab. **Nerve damage risk from nitrous is driven almost entirely by how much you inhale and how often, so a product that makes 300 gram sessions effortless moved a lot of people from occasional user to clinical case report in months rather than years.** That is the real danger of the trend.

## Quick answers

**Is Galaxy Gas safe?** No. The gas is food grade nitrous oxide, but the tank format encourages doses far beyond what whippet users reached, and dose is the main predictor of nerve injury. The FDA advised consumers not to inhale these products on March 14, 2025.

**How is a tank different from a whippet?** Volume and pressure. An 8 gram charger self limits your session. A 580 gram tank does not, and the pressure adds frostbite and lung injury risks chargers do not carry.

**How fast can nerve damage happen?** In a French cohort, median time from first use to neurological symptoms was 6 months, with some patients presenting in under a month.

**Will a normal B12 test tell me I am fine?** No. In a German cohort of 20 patients with confirmed nitrous nerve injury, serum B12 was normal in 95 percent. Ask for methylmalonic acid and homocysteine instead.

**Does flavoring make it worse?** No published inhalation toxicology exists on the additives, so nobody knows. The documented effect is behavioral: flavoring makes large volumes more palatable, and volume is what hurts you.

## What actually changed with tank products

Recreational nitrous existed for decades in 8 gram steel chargers. Buying a hundred is conspicuous, expensive per gram, and tedious to crack one at a time. Around 2023 and 2024, brands including Galaxy Gas, Baking Bad, Cosmic Gas, and MassGass began selling large flavored canisters in bright packaging through vape shops, gas stations, and Amazon. Same gas, different unit of sale.

Two things followed. Price per gram collapsed, removing cost as a brake on heavy use. And per session dose rose by an order of magnitude, because a tank has no natural stopping point the way a box of chargers does. Galaxy Gas halted its own direct online sales in September 2024 after the trend drew national attention, but competing brands remain widely available.

## The dose response is the whole story

Nitrous oxide irreversibly oxidizes the cobalt atom at the center of vitamin B12, shutting down methionine synthase and stalling myelin maintenance. Our post on [nitrous oxide and B12 nerve damage](/blog/nitrous-oxide-b12-nerve-damage.html) covers the mechanism in detail. Short version: every gram you inhale permanently disables some of your active B12, your liver stores are the buffer, and tanks empty that buffer much faster.

The clinical data tracks this:

- **Largeau et al. 2022** (*European Journal of Neurology*, PMID 35460312) studied 20 hospitalized patients, median age 19. Median use was 100 cartridges per occasion, range 5 to 960. Higher cumulative dose correlated with more extensive limb damage (p = 0.042). The authors concluded there is no safe recreational exposure level.
- **Tshagharyan et al. 2025** (*Neurological Research and Practice*, PMID 40319266) reported 20 patients, median age 21, with mean daily use of 2,500 grams, roughly 312 balloons per day. All had sensory deficits, 85 percent gait disturbance, 73 percent spinal cord lesions on MRI.

A 2,500 gram daily habit is implausible with 8 gram chargers and trivially easy with tanks. This is case series evidence and no randomized trial will ever be run, but the biochemistry is unambiguous and the dose relationship holds across cohorts.

## Why your B12 test can read normal while your spinal cord is failing

Serum B12 measures total circulating cobalamin, including the molecules nitrous has already oxidized into uselessness. Inactivated B12 still shows up on the test. Two functional markers tell the real story: **methylmalonic acid (MMA)**, which rises when B12 dependent metabolism stalls, and **homocysteine**, which accumulates when methionine synthase is offline.

In Tshagharyan's cohort, serum B12 was normal in 95 percent of patients while homocysteine was elevated in 100 percent and MMA in 95 percent. **Meißner et al. 2025** (*Neurological Research and Practice*, PMID 39815374) found the same pattern across 23 patients at five German hospitals: B12 abnormal in only 35 percent, MMA in 95 percent, homocysteine in 89 percent. **Hassing et al. 2024** (*European Journal of Neurology*, PMID 37754673), a 70 patient series, found 91 percent of patients with normal B12 had elevated MMA.

If your B12 comes back at 400, that is not reassurance. Ask specifically for MMA and homocysteine.

## Early symptoms that mean stop now

These develop over days to weeks and are your only warning before damage gets harder to reverse:

- Tingling, numbness, or pins and needles in fingers and toes, usually symmetrical
- Feeling unsteady walking, especially in the dark or with eyes closed
- Electric shock sensation down the spine when you bend your neck forward (Lhermitte sign)
- Clumsiness with buttons, keys, or a phone screen
- Later: leg weakness, stiffness, bladder problems

Sensory symptoms come first in nearly every published series. If your hands or feet are tingling after tank use, stop and get evaluated.

## The tank itself: frostbite, barotrauma, and hypoxia

**Frostbite.** Nitrous leaves a pressurized tank extremely cold. A 2025 review in the *European Burn Journal* (PMID 40137010) found most published nitrous frostbite cases clustered in 2022 to 2024, commonly on the inner thighs from resting the tank there. Some required excision and skin grafting. Inhaling from the valve can freeze the lips, mouth, and airway.

**Barotrauma.** Inhaling straight from a pressurized tank has caused pneumothorax and pneumomediastinum, gas forced through torn lung tissue into the chest cavity. A balloon prevents this by dropping the gas to ambient pressure first.

**Hypoxia.** Nitrous displaces oxygen and blunts the panic reflex that would make you pull away. Sustained inhalation from a mask, a bag over the head, or a sealed car is what kills people. US nitrous poisoning deaths rose from 23 in 2010 to 156 in 2023, 1,240 total among people aged 15 to 74, per **Yockey and Hoopsick 2025** in *JAMA Network Open* (PMID 40736738). Read honestly: that rise ran from 2010 to 2018 then plateaued through 2023, so the tank era's mortality effect is not yet in published data.

## Where regulation stands

The FDA issued a consumer advisory on March 14, 2025 titled "FDA Advises Consumers Not to Inhale Nitrous Oxide Products," naming Galaxy Gas, Whip-It!, Baking Bad, Cosmic Gas, HOTWHIP, InfusionMax, MassGass, and Miami Magic, and citing reports of nerve damage, blood clots, loss of consciousness, paralysis, psychiatric effects, and death. It added ExoticWhip, FastGas, and NITROX on June 4, 2025, and said the warning covers any flavor in any size canister, tank, or charger.

Louisiana and Michigan restricted recreational sales in 2024, and Alabama, California, and Washington added restrictions in 2025. Federally these products stay legal as food propellant, which is why they are still on gas station shelves.

## Harm reduction if you are going to use anyway

- **Use a balloon. Never inhale from the tank valve.** Highest value rule here. It removes the pressure and cold injury risk entirely.
- **Set a hard ceiling before you start** and count. A few balloons is a categorically different exposure from a session that empties a tank.
- **Sit or lie down.** Fainting is common, and falls are a leading cause of nitrous ER visits.
- **Never in a car, a closed room, a tent, or with anything over your head.** Oxygen displacement is what causes deaths.
- **Space sessions by weeks, not hours.** Frequency drives cumulative dose.
- **Get MMA and homocysteine checked** if you use more than occasionally, not just serum B12.
- **Supplement, but do not treat it as armor.** Oral [methylcobalamin B12](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=methylcobalamin+b12&tag=ravewellness01-20) maintains baseline stores, and low baseline B12 is the biggest risk multiplier. It has never been shown to prevent nitrous myeloneuropathy in heavy users.
- **Do not stack with alcohol, ketamine, GHB, or opioids.** Sedation plus hypoxia is how people stop breathing. Check combinations with our [drug interaction checker](/interactions.html).

## Recovery if you catch it early

Treatment is abstinence plus intramuscular hydroxocobalamin, not oral cyanocobalamin. Outcomes in the Hassing series (PMID 37754673) at three months: 31 percent fully recovered, 50 percent had mild residual symptoms, 19 percent still had disabling deficits. In Meißner's cohort, four of seven patients with follow up showed no meaningful improvement.

Roughly a third recover completely, and they are overwhelmingly the ones who stopped early. Tell your doctor exactly what you used and how much, because they will not order MMA and homocysteine if they think you have an unexplained neuropathy.
