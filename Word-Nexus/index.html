How to Play

Each puzzle is a 3×3 grid where every row, column, and diagonal points to the same center word.

The four dark corners are given. Fill in the four blank edge tiles by finding the word that semantically bridges the two corners on its axis.

Once all four edges are solved, the center unlocks — guess the word every axis converges on.

You have 5 mistakes per puzzle. New puzzle every day at midnight your time.
