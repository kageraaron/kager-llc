Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This page describes how Word Nexus ("the Site," "we," "our") handles information when you play the daily puzzle.

What we store on your device

Word Nexus is a static website with no user accounts and no backend database. To make the daily puzzle work, we use your browser's localStorage to keep:

  • Your current and longest streak
  • The date you last played and your progress on today's puzzle
  • Your win count, mistake distribution, and best time

This data never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data will reset it. We do not sync it across devices.

Cookies and analytics

We may use a privacy-respecting analytics tool (such as Plausible or Cloudflare Web Analytics) to count anonymous page views and understand which puzzles are popular. These tools do not use cookies and do not collect personal data.

Advertising

Word Nexus displays advertising provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also visit aboutads.info to opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising.
  • For users in the EU/UK, we display a consent banner before non-essential ad cookies are set.

Children

The Site is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The localStorage entries described above are technical and contain no personal information.

Third-party services

We load the following third-party resources, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Fonts — for typography
  • Google AdSense — for advertising

Your rights

Because we don't collect personally identifiable information server-side, there is no account to delete. You can clear all data the Site has stored by clearing your browser's site data for this domain. For ad-related data, use the opt-out links above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. The "last updated" date above always reflects the most recent revision.
